## Key findings

1. **Search is barely a channel** — 19 clicks in 3 months, 84% to the homepage.
2. **Brand-dependent, and not #1 for its own name** — `dot creative` sits at **position 11.5 (page 2)**; competing with dotYeti, Dot Foundry, Dot & Company, Dot Creative Kft, etc.
3. **Invisible for money terms** — "creative agency ontario" (pos 66), "design agency ontario" (pos 59), "full service creative agency" (pos 26): impressions but no clicks.
4. **Weakest in the home market** — Canada position 22 (page 3) vs US position 8.
5. **Rankings slipping** — avg position worsened from ~9 (April) to ~18.5 (July).
6. **Two quick wins** — `/brief` (page-1, 0 clicks → fix title/meta); the blog (position 39 → needs SEO help).
