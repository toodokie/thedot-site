## Instagram + Facebook caption
Hiring a foreign worker and hearing the letters "LMIA" for the first time?

Here is the short version: a Labour Market Impact Assessment is how you show that hiring abroad will not displace a Canadian worker. Approved, it lets your candidate apply for a work permit tied to your business.

Wondering if your role needs one? Book a consultation and we will walk you through it.
