# Reel on-screen copy for review

Five short Reels, four frames each. This is exact visible copy before visual proofs or animation. The article carries the detailed setup, prompts, connections and campaign example. The viewer chooses Canva, ChatGPT with Canva, or Claude Design with their usual video editor. A five-minute routine is the aim for a simple Reel after setup, with footage and offer already available.

## 01 / Find it, check it, use it

1. **Make polished Reels for your business, without the weekly scramble.** Set up your brand once. Use the tool you already like.
2. **Ask your AI to find the pieces.** Search only the files you give it: logo, colour codes, fonts, voice and three posts worth repeating.
3. **You decide what is official.** Check the latest versions, font rights, real claims and private material. Then save the approved pack.
4. **Choose your route.** Canva. ChatGPT with Canva. Or Claude Design with your video editor. Reuse a Reel format, review on your phone, post. Full setup in bio.

## 02 / ChatGPT

1. **ChatGPT user? Let it find your brand first.**
2. **Ask for an inventory of connected files.** You approve the current assets. Put the pack and your voice rules in a ChatGPT Project.
3. **Connect Canva in Settings → Plugins.** “Find our approved Reel design. Copy it and use this Friday pickup script.”
4. **Finish in Canva.** Add real footage. Check the offer, type, captions and phone crop. Full prompt and setup in the article.

## Interlude / Anonymous client review portal

1. **One client review. Too many tabs.**
2. **Video here. Copy edits there. Approval in email.**
3. **We brought the review into one place.** Watch, edit the words, approve.
4. **One clear decision.** The anonymous case study on The Dot site.

Use only non-identifying demo visuals until client permission is documented. No client data, copy, names or logos in the proof. Link the last frame to the site only after the case page is public.

## 03 / Claude Design

1. **Claude Design? Give your Reels a recognizable look.**
2. **Give it the approved brand pack.** Logo, colours, fonts and three examples. Save a design system if your plan allows.
3. **Ask for a 9:16 storyboard.** Four scenes, title style and end card. Use stills from your real footage.
4. **Finish in your video editor.** Add real clips. Check the offer and phone crop. Canva is optional. Full guide in bio.

## 04 / Canva

1. **Already live in Canva? Stay there.**
2. **Choose the approved files.** Add your current logo, colours and fonts to Brand Kit. Canva AI can use your kit and existing designs where available.
3. **Save one 9:16 Reel master.** Headline, captions, end card. Next week, duplicate and swap the footage and message.
4. **Review at phone size. Export. Post.** Full setup in the article.

## 05 / Keep it current

1. **Your saved brand needs a current version.**
2. **Ask your agent to find old assets.** You choose what to replace. Keep one dated, approved pack.
3. **Update the tool you use.** Canva Brand Kit, ChatGPT Project or Claude design system. Refresh your Reel master too.
4. **Run a five-minute check each month.** Logo, type, offer, facts, crop. Then make the next post.
