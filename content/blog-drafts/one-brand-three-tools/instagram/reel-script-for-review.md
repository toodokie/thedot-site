# Reel on-screen copy for review

Five short Reels, four frames each. This is exact visible copy before visual proofs or animation. The article carries the detailed setup, prompts, connections and campaign example. A five-minute routine is the aim for a simple, repeatable Reel after the brand and template are set up, with footage and offer already available.

## 01 / The five-minute Reel routine

1. **Your brand. Your five-minute Reel routine.** Set up once. Make polished posts in the tool you already use.
2. **Save the brand basics.** Correct logo, exact colours, voice, three approved examples. Fonts you can use: licensed brand fonts or free Google Fonts.
3. **Choose your setup.** Canva: Brand Kit and Reel template. ChatGPT: Project and brand rules. Claude: design system and visual references.
4. **Then repeat.** Topic → draft → real footage → phone-size review → post. We show each setup next. Full guide in bio.

## 02 / ChatGPT

1. **Using ChatGPT for your business posts?** Teach it your brand once.
2. **Create a Project.** Add your brand pack. Set your audience, offer, voice and words to avoid in Project instructions.
3. **Give it a repeatable job.** “Write a 10-second Reel about Friday pickup. One hook, three short scenes, one caption. Use our brand rules. Flag facts to check.”
4. **Pick a draft. Check the facts.** Use your footage. If Canva is your editor, connect it and finish the Reel there. Full steps in the article.

## 03 / Claude Design

1. **Using Claude for your next Reel?** Give it the look, not just the brief.
2. **Set up your design system.** Add the brand guide, real examples and the right logo. Check the fonts and colours it learned.
3. **Ask for two vertical story directions.** Same Friday pickup offer. Different ways to use the headline and real product footage.
4. **Choose one. Finish in your video editor.** Check type, crop and offer at phone size. Full setup in the article.

## 04 / Canva

1. **Using Canva for business posts?** Build the Reel you can reuse.
2. **Set up Brand Kit.** Add your logo, exact colours and fonts you can use.
3. **Make one 9:16 Reel template.** Save the title treatment, caption position and end card. Next time, swap the footage and message.
4. **Review at phone size. Export. Post.** Save the approved version as your starting point. Full steps in the article.

## 05 / Keep it current

1. **A fast workflow needs a current brand.**
2. **Keep one dated source pack.** One owner updates the logo, offer, fonts and examples when they change.
3. **Refresh the tool you use.** Your Canva kit, ChatGPT Project or Claude design system. Update the Reel template too.
4. **Run a five-minute check each month.** Logo, type, offer, facts, crop. Then make the next post.
