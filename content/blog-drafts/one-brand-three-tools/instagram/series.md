## 02 / ChatGPT

**Cover:** `YOUR BRAND VOICE NEEDS MORE THAN A PROMPT.`

**Slide 2:** `BUILD A PROJECT.` Add your dated brand pack. In Project instructions, state the audience, offer, tone, exact product names, phrases to avoid and what needs human approval.

**Slide 3:** `ASK FOR CONTRAST.` Try: “Give me three genuinely different campaign angles. One promise, one headline and one visual thought for each. Flag anything you would have to invent.”

**Slide 4:** `CHOOSE THE ANGLE. EDIT THE WORDS.` A Project keeps context together. It does not know which idea your business should stand behind. Full workflow in the article. Link in bio.

**Caption:**

If you paste “make it on-brand” into a blank chat, the tool has to guess what your brand is.

Put your current brand pack in a ChatGPT Project. Write the rules that stay true across campaigns. Ask for distinct ideas and make it flag missing facts. Then choose and edit the idea yourself.

The article also shows where the Canva plugin fits. Link in bio.

Source: OpenAI, “Projects in ChatGPT”; Canva, “Connect your Canva account to ChatGPT.”

## 03 / Claude Design

**Cover:** `SHOW THE SYSTEM. THEN EXPLORE.`

**Slide 2:** `GIVE IT VISUAL EVIDENCE.` Upload approved logos, a brand guide, a strong deck or a design system codebase. Check the extracted colours, fonts and patterns before publishing the system.

**Slide 3:** `COMPARE DIRECTIONS.` Use the same approved headline. Ask for a spare typographic poster and a product-led composition. Check reading order, crop, logo and product truth.

**Slide 4:** `A PREVIEW IS A PROPOSAL.` Keep the strongest idea. Send it to Canva where supported, or rebuild against your Brand Kit. Full steps in the article. Link in bio.

**Caption:**

“Modern, premium, clean” is too broad to reproduce a brand. Claude Design works better with real examples and an extracted design system you have checked.

Give it the chosen campaign angle, then compare two different visual ideas. Inspect the actual composition before moving one forward. Canva export and connector availability vary by account.

Full workflow in the article. Link in bio.

## 04 / Canva

**Cover:** `THIS IS WHERE THE DRAFT BECOMES AN ASSET.`

**Slide 2:** `SET UP THE BRAND KIT.` Add correct logo variants, exact colours, fonts, guidelines and approved templates. Check the kit on a real page, not just in settings.

**Slide 3:** `FINISH AT REAL SIZE.` Replace generated product imagery with the real photo. Check the logo, crop, type licence, facts and call to action. Export the actual format you need.

**Slide 4:** `SAVE THE APPROVED LAYOUT.` Reuse a proven structure. Change one meaningful variable per variation. Full workflow in the article. Link in bio.

**Caption:**

Canva is the finishing table in this workflow. A Brand Kit makes the right assets available. An approved template makes a good layout repeatable. Neither decides whether today's headline and image are right.

Before export, inspect the piece at phone size and verify every product detail. Save the approved version so the next campaign starts from a real example.

Full workflow in the article. Link in bio.

Sources: Canva, “Set up Brand Kits” and “Design with Brand Kits and Brand Templates.”

## 05 / Keep it current

**Cover:** `YOUR BRAND SYSTEM IS A LIVING FILE.`

**Slide 2:** `UPDATE THE SOURCE FIRST.` Name an owner. Date the brand pack. Record changes to offer, audience, logo, type, palette and approved examples. Remove obsolete files.

**Slide 3:** `THEN UPDATE ALL THREE.` Canva Brand Kit. ChatGPT Project. Claude Design system. Run one identical test brief through each and compare the output to the source.

**Slide 4:** `AGILITY IS CONTROLLED CHANGE.` Keep what makes the brand recognizable. Adapt the campaign to the moment. Recheck before the next batch. Full checklist in the article. Link in bio.

**Caption:**

The drift usually begins quietly. A new logo lands in Canva. The old one stays in a ChatGPT Project. Claude still has last season's deck. A week later, your campaign has three versions of the brand.

Give one person ownership of the source pack and date every change. Update each tool, then run the same test brief through all three. That is how you move quickly without accumulating contradictions.

The full setup, campaign example and monthly check are in the article. Link in bio.
