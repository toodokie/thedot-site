# Instagram carousel alt text

Use one entry per slide when uploading to @thedotcreativeagency. The text in quotation marks is visible on the slide. All slides use high contrast cream and charcoal with restrained acid yellow. The recurring header reads “The Dot / Field Notes” and the footer reads “One Brand / Three Tools.”

## Episode 01, the map

1. Cream cover with three overlapping cards labelled ChatGPT, Claude and Canva. Headline: “One brand. Three tools. One source of truth.”
2. Checklist of five brand pack ingredients: correct logos, exact colours, licensed type, audience and offer, and approved examples.
3. Three cards assign jobs: ChatGPT for angles and copy, Claude for visual direction, Canva for the editable finish.
4. Dark panel asks the three second check: right logo, clear message and real product. Points to the full article in the bio link.

## Episode 02, ChatGPT

1. Charcoal cover with a stack of white brand voice papers. Headline: “Your brand voice needs more than a prompt.”
2. Three steps for a ChatGPT Project: add the dated brand pack, set audience and tone, and name claims a person must check.
3. Three white and yellow cards suggest asking for three distinct angles, a promise and headline for each, and a flag for invented facts.
4. Dark panel reminds readers that a Project holds context while the business chooses and edits the angle.

## Episode 03, Claude Design

1. Cream cover compares two visual mockups, one typographic and one product led. Headline: “Show the system. Then explore.”
2. Checklist to give Claude logos, a guide and strong examples, then check the extracted system.
3. Cards compare a typographic poster with a product led composition using one approved headline.
4. Dark panel says to check crop, hierarchy and truth, then take the best idea to Canva.

## Episode 04, Canva

1. Cream cover shows an editable card with corner handles and a product placeholder. Headline: “This is where the draft becomes an asset.”
2. Checklist to add logo variants, colours, fonts, guidelines and approved templates, then test the kit.
3. Cards say to use the real product photo, check crop, licence and facts, then export the needed format.
4. Dark panel says to reuse an approved layout and change one thing per variation.

## Episode 05, keep it current

1. Charcoal cover shows three version strips, with the approved version highlighted in yellow. Headline: “Your brand system is a living file.”
2. Checklist to name an owner, date the brand pack, record changes and remove obsolete examples.
3. Three cards name the systems to update: Canva Brand Kit, ChatGPT Project and Claude Design system.
4. Dark panel says to run one identical test brief, keep recognition and adapt the message.
