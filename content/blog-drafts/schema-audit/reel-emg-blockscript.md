## Blocks

**Block 1 — Hook (0–10s)**
- **Narration:** "Your website already has schema. That is not the win."
- **Visual:** a website card on the cream canvas, faint JSON-LD code glowing behind it; a big ink label stamps on: **"SCHEMA ✓"**.
- **On-screen annotation:** the check turns, a yellow question mark hooks onto it → label flips to **"…but is it helping?"**

**Block 2 — The real question (10–20s)**
- **Narration:** "Having schema and having schema that works are two different things. The job is an audit, not an install."
- **Visual:** the word **INSTALL** gets a yellow strike-through; **AUDIT · CORRECT · ENRICH** slides in beneath.
- **On-screen annotation:** three ink chips animate in, yellow underline sweeps under them.

**Block 3 — Checks 1–2: inventory + validate (20–30s)**
- **Narration:** "First, list what is already there. Then validate it. Two tools, two different jobs."
- **Visual:** two label cards side by side: **"Schema.org Validator"** and **"Google Rich Results Test"**.
- **On-screen annotation:** a bracket `}` groups them with a note: **"different jobs — not interchangeable"**; yellow accent on the bracket.

**Block 4 — Checks 3–4: type fit + visible truth (30–40s)**
- **Narration:** "Does the type match the page? And does every marked-up fact match what a visitor actually sees?"
- **Visual:** a service-page card; a callout crosses out **"Product"** and points to **"Organization / LocalBusiness"**.
- **On-screen annotation:** **"Organization ≠ Product"**; yellow underline under **"must match the page."**

**Block 5 — Checks 5–6: connect + deliver (40–50s)**
- **Narration:** "Connect repeated entities with stable IDs. And make sure a crawler can actually reach the live page."
- **Visual:** a small connected diagram: **WebSite → Organization → LocalBusiness**, joined by ink lines with a **stable `@id`** tag; a crawler/spider mark travels the line and reaches the page.
- **On-screen annotation:** yellow dot pulses at each node as it connects.

**Block 6 — Myth-bust (50–60s)**
- **Narration:** "There is no special AI schema. It will not buy you a ranking. It gives machines cleaner facts."
- **Visual:** big ink headline **"NO SPECIAL AI SCHEMA"**; small credit line **"— Google's own guidance."**
- **On-screen annotation:** a yellow underline snaps under **"cleaner facts, not a shortcut."**

**Block 7 — CTA (60–70s)**
- **Narration:** "Audit, correct, enrich. Not just install. See where yours stands. Free check, link in bio."
- **Visual:** end card — **AUDIT · CORRECT · ENRICH**, The Dot logo + dot device, **"free AI-visibility check → link in bio."**
- **On-screen annotation:** yellow dot lands as the period.
