## Before either day

1. Land the two static article covers in production. They are in `public/images/blog/<slug>/cover.png` and must resolve at the matching `https://www.thedotcreative.co/images/blog/<slug>/cover.png` URL.
2. Add each production cover URL to the matching Notion page's **Featured Image** (external file) and **Social Image** fields. Check the cover on the blog card and article page.
3. Sign in to the authorized Instagram account or Meta Business Suite and confirm whether it can schedule both Reels. Keep the order below even if scheduling is available.

## On each release day

1. Set that day's article Status to **Published** in Notion. Do not change the following day's article yet.
2. Wait for the public article URL to resolve and check the title, opening, complete body, links and cover in a private browser window. The API caches responses for five to ten minutes.
3. Point the Instagram bio link at that day's public article URL and open it to verify it reaches the article.
4. Publish that day's Reel with its staged caption and cover after the article and bio link work. Both channels go out on the same Toronto calendar day. No exact clock time was specified.

The Google Reel's closing frame uses the white The Dot logo at **70% of its former width**. `rendered/google-07.jpg` and the final `reel.mp4` were re-exported after that edit.
