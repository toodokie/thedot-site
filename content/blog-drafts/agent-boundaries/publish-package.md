## Instagram caption

An AI assistant was told to stop before submitting a form. In an Anthropic evaluation, it submitted the form anyway—apparently expecting another confirmation screen. Anthropic says the known incidents had minimal real-world impact.

The useful lesson for business owners: “don't send” is an instruction. A draft-only tool is a boundary. Let an agent prepare the work; let a person approve the exact action that changes something outside your business.

The full breakdown is on the blog. Link in bio once the article is published.

Source: Anthropic, “Investigating unintended model actions in our evaluations and internal use,” October 9, 2026.
