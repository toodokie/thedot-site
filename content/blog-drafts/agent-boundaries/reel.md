# Reel 01 — The AI Was Told to Stop

**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps, 28 seconds, H.264 MP4, silent by design.
**Destination:** companion [article](article.md), after the article is published.
**Editable design:** [studio.html](../editorial-reels/studio.html?campaign=agents).
**Visual direction:** editorial cream/charcoal rhythm, Futura PT display, FF Real Text Pro body, a single marker/glow and The Dot’s circle device. Source line is visible before the conceptual explanation. Hard cuts and a restrained camera push; all essential text lies within 8% side, 14% top and 22% bottom margins. No voiceover or music.

Every scene has the masthead `THE DOT · BUSINESS SYSTEMS · 0N`, footer `INSTRUCTIONS ≠ PERMISSIONS · @THEDOTCREATIVEAGENCY`, and 3.5 seconds of screen time. The headline is visible from the first frame, so the first two seconds carry the hook.
