# Reel 02 — Google Changed Its Algorithm. Now What?

**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps, 28 seconds, H.264 MP4, silent by design.
**Destination:** companion [article](article.md), after the article is published.
**Editable design:** [studio.html](../editorial-reels/studio.html?campaign=google).
**Visual direction:** sister layout to Reel 01, with a diagnostic chart and ruled data list instead of a form. Cream/charcoal alternation, Futura PT/FF Real Text Pro, one yellow data marker. Safe area: 8% side, 14% top, 22% bottom. Hard cuts plus restrained camera push.
