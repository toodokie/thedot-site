# Reel: "The ChatGPT Test" (AI visibility)

Goal: **reach a new audience** (reels are pushed to non-followers). Format proves the problem on screen, so it stops the scroll and is inherently shareable. **No filming.**

- **Platform:** IG Reels (primary) → repurpose to YouTube Shorts + FB Reels
- **Specs:** 9:16, 1080×1920, **15-19s**, MP4, on-screen text burned in (most watch muted)
- **Build:** phone screen-recording + text overlays in CapCut (or Canva). Trending audio.
- **From:** @thedotcreativeagency

---

## STEP 0 — capture the screen recording first
On your phone, open ChatGPT, start iOS screen recording, and run ONE real query:

> **"Who are the best florists in Toronto?"**

Let it answer (it names ~3-5). That clip is your reel spine. (Swap "florists" for any everyday category that gives a clean short list, just **don't pick web design** — you don't want to be the answer here. A category viewers project their own business onto works best.)

Optional second clip: screen-record the free tool at **thedotcreative.co/tools/ai-visibility** for the ending.

---

## THE HOOK (first 2 seconds = everything)
Primary:
> **"I asked AI to recommend a business in Toronto. It only picked three."**

Alternates to A/B:
- "Your customers are asking AI who to hire. Watch what it says."
- "There are thousands of florists in Toronto. AI names three. Here's why that matters for you."

Put the hook as **big on-screen text over the first frame of the screen-rec.**

---

## STORYBOARD (shot by shot)

| Time | Visual | On-screen text |
| :- | :- | :- |
| 0-2.5s | ChatGPT open, query being typed | **"I asked AI to recommend a business in Toronto."** |
| 2.5-6s | AI generating the answer | "A customer describes what they need..." |
| 6-11s | AI's answer: the 3-5 named businesses (slow scroll / highlight them) | **"It picks three. Just three."** → then → "Thousands of businesses. The customer sees three." |
| 11-15s | Bold branded card (warm white, dot-grid, yellow) OR stay on screen | **"If AI doesn't name yours, you don't exist to that customer."** |
| 15-19s | Quick flash of the free self-check tool, then logo/handle end card | "45% now use AI to find local businesses (was 6%). **See if it names you → link in bio**" |

Keep it **tight**, shorter reels finish more often, and completion rate drives reach.
