Most fail

This is where new clients actually discover you: by describing a problem, not a name. Miss here and you are invisible at the exact moment someone is choosing who to call.

Bonus — Can it read your site?

Readability

Point the AI at your own page and see if it understands you. Use a tool that can open links (Perplexity always does; in ChatGPT, turn on search). If the summary comes back vague or wrong, that is a structure problem, not a you problem, and it is the fixable kind.

One more move

After any answer, ask the AI a follow-up: "What sources are you basing that on?" It reveals which pages it is actually reading. If it cites your competitors and not you, that is your gap, in one line.
