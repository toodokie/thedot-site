# The AI Member of The Dot — persona & art-direction brief

Production brief for the recurring AI host of The Dot Creative's video content. Keep this locked so every episode reads as the same character.

## Identity
- **Positioning:** "the AI member of The Dot Creative." No proper name. The introduction is the disclosure.
- **Nature:** a stylized AI character **heavily inspired by Anastasia** (founder). Her look and vibe as the reference, heightened and stylized into a character, not a 1:1 digital twin. This keeps it honest (a character inspired by the founder, not a deepfake of the real person) and consent-clean (her own likeness, she directs it).
- **Role:** hosts The Dot's AI-visibility and marketing explainers, and the recurring fact-check segment **"AI, Fact-Checked."**

## Disclosure (locked)
- **In-content open:** "I'm the AI member of The Dot..."
- **Operating rule (no on-screen disclosure tagline):** the host only presents claims the human team has verified. Disclosure is the in-content intro ("I'm the AI member of The Dot"), not a sign-off line.
- **Platform:** Meta AI-content label ON.
- **In-frame cue:** a small acid-yellow dot signature (pin, earring, or graphic) — the brand device doubling as a subtle AI marker.

## Aesthetic — elegant but bold (grungy editorial noir)
Reference: **Saint Laurent noir + grittier COS / Acne editorial + film-grain chiaroscuro portraiture**, cross-bred with the agency's own social language (see the "FREE COSTS" and "BRANDING" posts: dark editorial grounds, halftone cut-outs, glitch, bold *sparse* yellow). Keywords: **minimal, grungy, decadent, bold. Elegant but bold.**

- **Mood:** moody editorial, chiaroscuro, film grain. Decadent but restrained. Elegant with grit and attitude, not pretty-safe.
- **Treatment:** film grain + **halftone / dithered** texture. The character can appear as an **animated cut-out sticker** (halftone edges, collage-style) — the technique used for the hands in the "FREE COSTS" post.
- **Wardrobe:** still sculptural, tailored, luxe (silk, velvet, aged brass) but darker and moodier. Decadent, not camel-coat clean.
- **Palette — old newspaper, careful and sparse:** halftone black-and-white, newsprint greys, aged paper. Grounds can go **deep charcoal / near-black** (this is a SOCIAL surface, not the website UI). Acid-yellow `#daff00` is the **single, sparse, bold accent** — a marker highlight, one cut-out, a headline hit. Never a wash, never everywhere.
- **Set / ground:** dark charcoal or aged-newsprint, generous negative space, editorial masthead labels (e.g. "THE DOT · AI, FACT-CHECKED · 01"). Sparse, confident.
- **Camera / light:** dramatic chiaroscuro side-light, deep shadow, medium / medium-close editorial portrait. A noir campaign figure who happens to speak.
- **Hair / beauty:** effortless, matte, undone. High-contrast, grainy.

## Consistency kit (this is what makes it a brand asset, not a one-off)
- Lock a character reference sheet: same face, styling language, palette, and set language across every episode.
- Save the character reference in the generation tool; keep a fixed style prompt plus reference images.

## Production notes
- **The look is won in the still.** Generate a strong editorial keyframe first (in the brand palette), then animate and lip-sync from it.
- **Tools:** prototype the talking head in CapCut (free) to test look/script/timing; move to Higgsfield Speak for polished, consistent episodes if CapCut is not premium enough for this aesthetic. Do the final edit and burned-in captions in CapCut either way.
- **Blog authorship:** stays human-authored (The Dot Creative). The AI member is the IG/video presenter only. *(Still your call to confirm.)*
